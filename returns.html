RETURNS & EXCHANGES

We have a 30-day return policy, which means you have 30 days after receiving your item to request a return.

To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with all original packaging intact. You’ll also need the receipt or proof of purchase.

To start a return, you can contact us at snsfragranceid@gmail.com . Please include your order number, the name of the product, and the reason for your return.

If your return is accepted, we will provide instructions on how and where to send your package. Items sent back to us without first requesting a return will not be accepted.

You can always contact us for any return questions at snsfragranceid@gmail.com .

DAMAGES AND ISSUES

Please inspect your order upon reception and contact us immediately if the item is defective, damaged, leaking, or if you receive the wrong item, so that we can evaluate the issue and make it right.

For damaged packages, please provide clear photographs of the outer packaging, shipping label, product box, bottle and the affected area when contacting us.

NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS

Due to hygiene and product safety considerations, certain items may not be eligible for return.

  • Perfume or fragrance products that have been opened or used.
  • Products with removed, damaged or altered seals where the product can no longer be resold safely.
  • Products damaged after delivery due to improper handling or storage.
  • Gift cards and other non-returnable items where applicable.

If you are unsure whether your item is eligible for return, please contact us before sending the product back.

EXCHANGES

We only replace items if they are defective, damaged, or if the wrong product was sent.

If you need to exchange an eligible item, please contact us first at snsfragranceid@gmail.com .

Exchanges are subject to product availability. If the requested replacement is unavailable, we may offer an alternative resolution where applicable.

REFUNDS

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you whether the return has been approved or rejected.

If approved, the refund will be processed to the original payment method, subject to the applicable payment provider's processing time.

Please note that it may take additional time for your bank or payment provider to reflect the refund in your account.

RETURN SHIPPING

Unless the item received was defective, damaged, or incorrect, return shipping costs may be the responsibility of the customer.

If the return is the result of an error on our part or a product that arrived damaged or defective, SNS Fragrance may cover the applicable return shipping cost.

ORDER CANCELLATION

If you need to cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible after placing your order.

We cannot guarantee that a cancellation request can be fulfilled once an order has been processed or shipped.

Important: Please do not send a product back to SNS Fragrance before contacting us and receiving return instructions. Unauthorized returns may not be accepted or processed.
